FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Our travel experts are a call or message away.

09612-787878All FAQs
How do I book a tour with Musafir?

Choose a package, pick your date and travellers, then pay a deposit online with bKash, Nagad, card or bank transfer. You get an instant e-voucher by email and SMS.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. International holidays and cruises can be paid in up to 4 installments. Selected bank cards also offer 0% EMI for 3–12 months.

Is Musafir a licensed travel agency?

Yes. We are IATA accredited, members of ATAB and TOAB, and registered with the Bangladesh Tourism Board (BTB-2214).

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations 30+ days before departure get a full refund minus non-refundable airline, visa and government fees. See the Refund Policy page for the full slab.

Do you help with visa processing?

Yes — document checklists, appointment booking, form filling and submission for 40+ countries. Our visa desk shows your application status in your account.

Are flights included in tour prices?

Packages marked "Flight included" have return economy tickets. Others are land-only and you can add flights during booking.
