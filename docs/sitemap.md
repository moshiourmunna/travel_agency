## Public-site page groups

- **Core:** home, about, team, careers, contact, FAQ, legal (terms, privacy, refund policy), photo credits, HTML sitemap, brand sheet and 404.
- **Catalogue:** tours (list + 16 details), destinations (12), themes (6), cruises (6), activities (12), hotels (8), flights, visa hub + 10 country pages; holiday themes hub at `holidays.html`.
- **Conversion:** booking checkout, booking confirmation, trip planner, custom tour, group tours, gift cards, insurance, compare, wishlist, offers, login, register and forgot-password.
- **Content:** blog (list + 6 posts), reviews and gallery.

## SEO files
- `site/sitemap.xml`: every indexable page with EN/BN `xhtml:link` alternates.
- `site/robots.txt`: disallows `/en/account/`, `/en/admin/`, the `/bn/` equivalents and the checkout.
