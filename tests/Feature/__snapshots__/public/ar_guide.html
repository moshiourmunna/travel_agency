الحج خطوة بخطوة

8 Dhul Hijjah – Ihram & Mina

Enter Ihram from your residence, recite Talbiyah, move to Mina and pray Zuhr to Fajr there.

9 Dhul Hijjah – Arafah

The pillar of Hajj. Stand in Arafat from Zuhr until sunset in dua; then go to Muzdalifah and collect pebbles.

10 Dhul Hijjah – Eid day

Stone Jamarat al-Aqabah (7), Qurbani, shave/trim hair, Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i.

11–12 (13) Dhul Hijjah – Tashreeq

Stone all three Jamarat daily after Zuhr, stay nights in Mina.

Farewell

Perform Tawaf al-Wada before leaving Makkah.

العمرة خطوة بخطوة

1. Ihram

Make intention at the Miqat, wear Ihram and recite Talbiyah.

2. Tawaf

Seven circuits around the Kaaba starting from the Black Stone.

3. Two rak'ah

Pray two rak'ah behind Maqam Ibrahim and drink Zamzam.

4. Sa'i

Seven rounds between Safa and Marwah.

5. Halq / Taqsir

Shave or trim the hair – your Umrah is complete.

للنساء

Ihram for women

Regular modest clothing; face and hands uncovered; no special colour.

Menstruation

All rites except Tawaf may be performed; wait for purity before Tawaf.

Group travel

Women above 45 may travel in an organised group – our female guide accompanies every group.
