تسجيل حج 2027: المواعيد والرسوم

Every year the Ministry of Religious Affairs announces the Hajj package and the registration schedule for private and government pilgrims. This article explains, step by step, what you need to do for Hajj 2027 (1448 AH).

1. Pre-registration

You need a valid NID and a mobile number. The pre-registration fee is paid through designated banks and you receive a tracking number.

2. Final registration

Once your serial is within the quota, you pay the remaining package price and submit your passport.

💡 Tip: keep your passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date.

3. Visa and training

Your agency submits your visa through Nusuk and invites you to pre-Hajj training sessions.
