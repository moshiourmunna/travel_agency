Since 2010

Your Sacred Journey, Our Trusted Care

Blessing Hajj Agency was founded with one aim: that every pilgrim from Bangladesh – from Dhaka to Teknaf – can perform Hajj and Umrah with ease, dignity and correct guidance. Today we serve thousands of pilgrims every year with our own teams in Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and Makkah.

🎯 Mission

Sincere service, transparent pricing, Sunnah-guided worship.

🌙 Vision

Bangladesh's most trusted pilgrimage partner.
