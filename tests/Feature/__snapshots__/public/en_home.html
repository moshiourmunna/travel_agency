Frequently Asked

Who can perform Hajj through your agency?

Any Bangladeshi Muslim with a passport valid for at least 6 months and completed pre-registration on the government Hajj portal. We handle registration for you.

How do installments work?

Pay a booking deposit (e.g. ৳ 50,000 for Hajj), then the balance in up to 4 scheduled installments before the final government payment deadline. Reminders come by SMS and WhatsApp.

Is Mahram required for women?

Current Saudi rules allow women above 45 to travel in an organised group; we advise per the latest Ministry of Religious Affairs circular.

What documents are required?

Passport, NID, recent photo (white background), meningitis vaccine certificate, and for Umrah a return ticket. Upload them from your dashboard.
