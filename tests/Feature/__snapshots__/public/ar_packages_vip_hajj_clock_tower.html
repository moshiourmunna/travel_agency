البرنامج اليومي

Day 1 – Dhaka → Jeddah

Ihram at Miqat, transfer to Makkah, Umrah with guide

Day 2–9 – Makkah

Daily prayers at Masjid al-Haram, lessons on Hajj rites, Ziyarah of Jabal al-Nour, Thawr, Arafat

Day 10–14 – Madinah

40 prayers at Masjid an-Nabawi, Rawdah visit (Nusuk permit), Quba, Uhud, Qiblatain

Day 15 – 8 Dhul Hijjah – Mina

Move to Mina – air-conditioned tents (Zone A)

Day 16 – 9 Dhul Hijjah – Arafah

Day of Arafah – Wuquf; night in Muzdalifah

Day 17–19 – Rami, Qurbani, Tawaf

Rami al-Jamarat, Qurbani (via official coupon), Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i

Day 21 – Farewell

Tawaf al-Wada and return flight to Dhaka
