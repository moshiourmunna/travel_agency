Frequently Asked Questions

Home / Frequently Asked Questions
Who can perform Hajj through your agency?

Any Bangladeshi Muslim with a passport valid for at least 6 months and completed pre-registration on the government Hajj portal. We handle registration for you.

How do installments work?

Pay a booking deposit (e.g. ৳ 50,000 for Hajj), then the balance in up to 4 scheduled installments before the final government payment deadline. Reminders come by SMS and WhatsApp.

Is Mahram required for women?

Current Saudi rules allow women above 45 to travel in an organised group; we advise per the latest Ministry of Religious Affairs circular.

What documents are required?

Passport, NID, recent photo (white background), meningitis vaccine certificate, and for Umrah a return ticket. Upload them from your dashboard.

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

Yes, as per our published refund policy; amounts already paid to airlines, Saudi Nusuk or government are deducted.

How long does an Umrah visa take?

Usually 5–7 working days after we receive a clear passport scan and photo.

Which payment methods do you accept?

bKash, Nagad, cards via SSLCommerz, bank transfer, and cash at our offices. You can also book now and pay later within the hold period.

Do you provide pre-Hajj training?

Yes, three sessions in Dhaka and online, led by our scholars, covering every rite step by step.

Can I combine Umrah with a Dubai or Istanbul stopover?

Yes, see our Tours & Ziyarah page for stopover packages.

Can I track my visa online?

Yes, use the tracker on the Visa page or your dashboard with your application ID.
